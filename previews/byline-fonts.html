Twelve faces for the maker’s plate

The plate is nine pixels of uppercase with a hair of tracking, sitting beside the app name. At that size the shape of a letter matters more than its personality, so each option is shown twice: once enlarged, so the letterforms are visible, and then at its real size on all four theme grounds, which is the only size that counts.

Tracking and weight are tuned per face rather than held identical. A condensed cut needs more air at nine pixels than a monospace does, and holding one value for all of them would flatter some and sink others.

As it stands

Each theme picks its own face, so the plate changes with the cabinet. Everything below replaces that with one face everywhere.
